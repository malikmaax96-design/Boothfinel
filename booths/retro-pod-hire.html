A booth built around the printer

Most open-air pods keep the printer in a separate case on the floor, which means a cable run, a table and a bit of mess. The Retro Pod puts the printer inside the column itself, so the whole booth is a single upright unit about 1.9 metres tall and half a metre square. A DSLR camera sits at eye level behind the front panel, with LED lighting built around it.

A session starts when a guest touches the screen. The pod runs a countdown, fires a sequence of shots and lays them out as a classic vertical photo strip, the kind you would have collected from a seaside booth years ago, but with a sharper image and a template designed for your event. The strips print from the slot in the pod, one for each person in the group, and the attendant hands them round.

The footprint: 1.5m by 1.5m and one socket

Because the printer lives inside the column, the Retro Pod needs less floor space than almost anything else we hire: a clear 1.5 metre by 1.5 metre area is enough for the pod and a small group in front of it. The unit measures approximately 1.9m high by 0.5m wide by 0.5m deep. Power comes from one standard socket, and there is no cabling across the floor apart from the single lead to the wall.

That tidy footprint suits venues where space is tight: a narrow hotel function room, a converted barn with pillars, a village hall with a stage taking up one end. Put it where there is a plain wall or room for a backdrop behind the guests, and ideally where the attendant can stand to one side without blocking a thoroughfare. The pod travels in cases and goes up stairs without trouble.

Six per shot, one strip each

The camera comfortably frames up to six guests, which keeps the strips readable; more than that and faces get small on a strip format. Each session takes about a minute including the print, so a steady hour sees around forty groups through. With a print for everyone in the shot, a group of four leaves with four strips, and the guest book gets its own copy from the attendant.

If you have a large guest list, the quick turnaround helps, and because sessions are unlimited guests often come back later in the night for a second go with different props. The attendant keeps the props tidy between groups and reloads the printer paper before it runs low, so the pace stays steady from the first guest to the last.

Photo strips, USB and email

The strip format is the reason people choose this pod. Each strip carries a column of frames with your names, the date or a logo printed along the bottom, and the attendant can print extras for the guest book. Prints are ready moments after the final frame, and there is no separate printer to walk to.

All photos from the night are copied to a USB stick for you to keep, and guests can email individual strips to themselves from the pod. Digital copies are included as standard so nothing is lost if a strip goes astray on the dance floor.

Vintage black and white or full colour are both available. The start screen can carry the same artwork, and a backdrop in your colours completes the look. Good to know

Retro Pod Hire FAQs

Why does the inbuilt printer matter?

It removes the separate printer case, the table it sits on and the cable between the two. The result is a single upright pod with one lead to the wall, which is tidier, safer in a busy room and quicker to set up. It also means the pod fits in a 1.5 metre square where other booths cannot.

How many prints come out of each session?

One photo strip for every guest in the shot, up to six per session. The attendant adds a further strip to the guest book so you keep a copy of every group. Sessions are unlimited during the hire, so guests can return as many times as they like.

Can the Retro Pod be used outside?

Only in a marquee or other covered area with a standard plug socket nearby. The DSLR, screen and inbuilt printer are not weatherproof, and the screen is hard to see in direct sunlight. A marquee reception with power is fine; an open lawn is not.

Will it fit through a standard door or up stairs?

Yes. The pod is about half a metre wide and travels in protective cases, so it passes through ordinary doorways and up a staircase without trouble. Let us know in advance if the room is on an upper floor with no lift so the attendant can plan the carry.

Do we get digital copies of the strips?

Yes. Every photo is saved and handed over on a USB stick at the end of the night, and guests can email strips to themselves from the pod during the event. The digital set is included in the package at no extra cost.

How long does setup take?

The attendant arrives 45 to 60 minutes before your hire window to position the pod, backdrop and props and test the printer. Because the printer is built in, setup is one of the quickest of our booths, and the time is never charged to you.
