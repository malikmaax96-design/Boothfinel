Meet the LCD SlimPod

At its heart the LCD SlimPod is an iPad-based touchscreen booth on a slim upright stand, not much wider than a lamp post. There is no cabinet, no curtain and no clutter, which is why it sits comfortably in rooms where a traditional enclosed booth simply would not fit. Guests step up to a backdrop or a plain wall, tap the screen to start, and the countdown begins.

A typical session runs like this: the screen shows a live preview, a short countdown fires, and the pod captures a series of shots. Guests can switch on filters, drop in virtual props or record a GIF or boomerang instead of a still. Two prints come out within seconds, and the attendant hands them over while the next group steps in. Nobody needs instructions, which keeps the queue moving all evening.

Size, footprint and where to put it

The pod itself measures 1.80m high, 0.36m wide and 0.59m deep, and it needs a floor area of about 1.5m by 2m once you allow for guests standing in front of it. One standard plug socket is all it takes to power the unit, the printer and the lighting. There is no ceiling height requirement beyond a normal room, so low-beamed barns and function suites with suspended ceilings are not a problem.

Where it goes matters more than how much space it takes. We suggest a spot that guests pass naturally, such as the edge of the dance floor or near the bar, with the backdrop against a solid wall so the camera is not looking into a window. Keep it away from the DJ speakers if you can, because the attendant needs to be heard when explaining how it works. If the venue is upstairs, that is fine; the pod comes apart into a couple of cases and goes up a staircase or in a lift.

Up to six people fit comfortably in a single shot, which covers most friendship groups and family clusters at a wedding. A session, from the first tap to prints in hand, takes about a minute, so over an hour you can expect somewhere between forty and fifty groups to pass through, allowing for the usual chatting and prop swapping. Across a three-hour hire that adds up to most of a typical wedding guest list having a go, often more than once.

Prints, GIFs and digital copies

Each session produces two prints, one for the guest book and one to take home, or two to split between friends. Prints are ready seconds after the final shot. Alongside the paper copies, every photo, GIF and boomerang is saved digitally and shared by email or straight to social media, so the night lives on after the prints have been pinned to a fridge.

The smart background removal is the feature people ask about most. The software separates guests from whatever is behind them and swaps in a digital scene, so there is no need for a green screen or even a physical backdrop. You can choose a beach, a city skyline, your wedding colours or a branded panel, and the attendant can change it between sessions if you like.

Good to know

LCD SlimPod Hire FAQs

Does the LCD SlimPod need a green screen?

No. The SlimPod uses smart background removal, which separates guests from whatever is behind them in software and drops in a digital scene. You can still have a physical backdrop or a green screen if you prefer a particular look, but neither is required for the digital backgrounds to work.

Can the SlimPod be set up upstairs?

Yes. The pod breaks down into a small number of carry cases, so our attendant can take it up a staircase or in a lift without difficulty. Let us know when booking if the room is on an upper floor and whether there is a lift, so we can allow the right amount of setup time.

Will it work outdoors?

Only in a marquee or a covered area with a power socket nearby. The touchscreen, printer and lighting are not weatherproof, and bright direct sunlight makes the screen hard to read, so an open garden is not suitable. A marquee, gazebo or covered terrace with a standard socket works well.

How long does setup take?

Our attendant arrives 45 to 60 minutes before the hire window begins and has the pod, backdrop, lighting and props ready before guests arrive. That setup time is included in the package and does not count towards the hours you have booked, and pack-down at the end is equally quick.

Can guests get digital copies of their photos?

Yes. Every photo, GIF and boomerang is saved and can be emailed to guests or shared to social media directly from the pod. You also receive digital copies of everything captured after the event, so the two prints per session are only part of what you take away.

Can we add extra hours on the night?

Often, yes. If the party is in full swing and the attendant has no other commitment that evening, extra time can usually be added for an additional charge. It is far safer to book the hours you think you need in advance, and we will tell you the cost of extending when we quote.
