The booth that lights up the room

The Inflatable Booth is a cube roughly 2.5 metres in every direction, inflated on site and lit from inside by LEDs so the whole structure glows. From across a marquee or a hotel ballroom it reads as a light installation; up close it is a spacious enclosed booth with a camera, screen and printer inside.

Guests enter through the doorway, gather in front of the camera and tap the screen. The booth counts down, captures a set of shots, and offers GIFs and filters alongside stills. Prints are produced for each person in the group, and the attendant manages the flow of people in and out so nobody is left outside wondering whose turn it is.

Space requirements: 4m by 4m and two sockets

This is the largest booth we hire, and it needs room to match. Allow a floor area of 4 metres by 4 metres and a ceiling height of at least 3 metres, because the inflated structure stands around 2.5 metres tall and needs clearance above it. Two standard plug sockets are required, and we bring our own extension leads.

Marquees, barns, sports halls and large function rooms all take it comfortably. Village halls and smaller hotel suites often do not, so measure before booking and send us a photo of the room if you are unsure. Ground-floor venues are easiest; the booth travels deflated in bags, but it is bulky, so tell us in advance about stairs, narrow corridors or a long walk from the car park.

Ten people, one photo

Up to ten people can be inside and in shot at the same time, which no other booth we offer can match. That makes it ideal for sports teams, hen parties, extended families and office departments who want one picture with everyone in it. A session takes a little over a minute given the time it takes ten people to get in and arrange themselves, so expect around thirty to thirty-five groups an hour. The glow attracts a crowd, so the booth rarely sits idle, and unlimited sessions mean nobody is turned away.

Prints for everyone, USB and email

Everyone in the shot receives a print on your template, so a group of ten produces ten prints. The guest book gets a copy as well, with space for a message. All images and GIFs are saved digitally, delivered on a USB stick at the end of the hire, and guests can email photos to themselves from the booth during the evening.

Colour matching and branding

The LED glow can be set to a colour that matches your event, whether that is the wedding palette, a company brand colour or a themed birthday. The print template is built around the same colours and carries your names, date or logo, and the welcome page on the screen can show your artwork. Props are included, with themed sets available on request.

Good to know

Inflatable Booth Hire FAQs

Can the Inflatable Booth be used outdoors?

Only inside a marquee or a fully covered structure with two standard sockets available. The booth must stay dry and needs a ceiling of at least 3 metres, which most marquees provide. It cannot be set up on open ground or in a gazebo exposed to wind and rain.

Will it fit in our venue?

You need a clear 4 metre by 4 metre floor area and 3 metres of ceiling height. Large function rooms, barns, sports halls and marquees are usually fine; smaller village halls and low-ceilinged hotel suites are often not. Send us the room dimensions or a photo and we will tell you straight away.

Can it go upstairs?

Sometimes. The booth travels deflated in bags, so it can be carried up stairs, but it is bulky and takes longer to move than our slim pods. Tell us about stairs, lifts and corridor widths when you book so we can plan the route and setup time.

Can the glow colour change during the event?

Yes. The LED colour is set to match your theme before guests arrive, and the attendant can change it during the hire if you want a different look for the evening party. Let us know your colours in advance so the booth is right from the start.

Do guests get digital copies?

Yes. Guests can email photos and GIFs to themselves from the booth on the night, and every image is handed over on a USB stick at the end of the hire. The prints for each guest are additional to the digital set, not instead of it.

Can we add extra hours?

Where the attendant has no later commitment, extra hours can normally be added at the rate quoted in advance. For big events we recommend booking a longer hire from the outset, because the queue for this booth tends to last the whole night.
