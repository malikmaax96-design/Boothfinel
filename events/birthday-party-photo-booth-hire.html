Milestone birthdays from 18 to 60

Milestone parties are where a booth works hardest. An 18th or 21st is full of friends who will queue for the booth all night and post everything straight away, so GIFs, boomerangs and email sharing matter. A 30th or 40th tends to mix friends, family and children, and the guest book becomes the thing the birthday person reads the next morning. At a 50th or 60th the booth is often the ice-breaker between groups who have not met, and prints for everyone mean nobody misses out.

Whatever the number on the cake, the template carries the name and the age, matched to the invitation, the balloons or the colour of the dress code, so the prints look like they belong to that party and no other.

Children's parties and the under-8s

A photo booth suits a children's party, including the under-8s, as long as an adult goes in with them. Small children need help holding props, standing in the right place and waiting for the countdown, and our attendant will prompt them but cannot be inside every shot. With a parent in the group it works beautifully: the Party Booth is enclosed and feels like a den, the Inflatable Booth glows in the party colour, and the prints go home in the party bags.

Garden parties, marquees and power

Home parties in a marquee are popular for summer birthdays, and a booth fits as long as three things are in place: a level, dry floor, a standard plug socket within reach, and enough covered space for the booth you choose. Power can run from the house on a proper outdoor extension lead; we bring our own leads but will ask in advance how far away the socket is. The booth has to stay under cover, so an open lawn without a marquee or gazebo is not an option.

Our slimmer pods need just 1.5m by 2m of floor, which fits in the corner of most marquees. The Inflatable Booth needs 4m by 4m and 3m of height, so it is better suited to a larger marquee or an open-sided barn.

Village halls and pub function rooms

Village halls, community centres and pub function rooms are the classic birthday venues and they suit a booth well. We check access, where the sockets are and what time the hall is unlocked, then set up before guests arrive. In a smaller function room the LCD SlimPod or Retro Pod keeps the footprint tidy and leaves room for the buffet and the dance floor; in a larger hall there is space for the Inflatable Booth or the Magic Mirror.

Themes, templates and a glow to match

Every package includes a custom template, and birthday parties are where it is most fun. A Hollywood 40th gets a gold-and-black strip with the name in lights; a 1980s 50th gets neon; a children's party gets dinosaurs or unicorns. The Inflatable Booth takes the theme further, because the colour of its LED glow can be matched to the party: pink for a 21st, blue and silver for a winter birthday, red and green for a Christmas party.

Birthdays that fall over Christmas and New Year are a good fit for a booth, because the party doubles as a seasonal get-together and the prints become the year's Christmas photo. December and New Year's Eve dates are in demand, so it pays to book early.

Combining the booth with a DJ, and how long to book

A booth and a DJ work well together provided they are not fighting for the same corner. We set the booth away from the speakers so the attendant can hear guests and the queue is not on the dance floor, and we are happy to coordinate with the DJ on when the booth opens and closes. Many hosts use the booth to fill the early part of the evening while the dance floor is empty and let the DJ carry the second half.

For a four-hour party, a three-hour hire is usually the right length: start it an hour in, once most guests have arrived, and finish an hour before the end when people start to drift. If your party is longer or has a late arrival time, we will suggest a start and finish to suit.

Good to know

Birthday Parties Photo Booth FAQs

Is a photo booth suitable for a child's birthday party?

Yes, including for under-8s, provided an adult goes in with the children. Our attendant prompts them and keeps things moving, but a parent in the shot helps with props and the countdown. The Party Booth and Inflatable Booth are the favourites for children's parties, and the prints go straight into the party bags.

Can the booth go in a marquee in our garden?

It can, as long as there is a level, dry floor, a standard socket within reach of an outdoor extension lead and enough covered space for the booth. The slim pods need 1.5m by 2m; the Inflatable Booth needs 4m by 4m with 3m of height. The booth cannot stand on an open lawn without cover.

How long should we book for a four-hour party?

Three hours is usually right. Start the booth about an hour after guests are invited, so the room is full when it opens, and finish an hour before the end when people start to leave. Setup and pack-down happen outside the hire time, so you are only paying for the hours the booth is running.

Can the Inflatable Booth match our party colours?

Yes. The colour of the Inflatable Booth's LED glow can be set to match the party theme, from pink for a 21st to red and green for a Christmas birthday. We match the print template to the same colours and the invitation, so the booth, the prints and the decorations all look like one set.

Does the booth work alongside a DJ?

Very well. We position the booth away from the speakers and off the dance floor, and coordinate with the DJ so the booth is busiest early on while the dance floor fills, then winds down as the music takes over. If you give us the DJ's details we will speak to them before the night.

Do you cover Christmas and New Year parties?

Yes. Birthdays over the festive period, Christmas parties and New Year's Eve are all covered, with templates themed to the season and the Inflatable Booth lit in red and green or silver and blue. December dates are the busiest of the year, so an early £80 deposit is the safest way to secure the night.
