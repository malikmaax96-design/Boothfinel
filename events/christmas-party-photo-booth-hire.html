Office and staff Christmas parties

For a staff party, the print template can carry your company logo alongside the festive design, so the photos people pin above their desks in January still say whose party it was. Send us the logo and any wording, such as the team name or a thank-you message for the year, and we will send a proof to approve before the night.

Friday and Saturday nights in December are claimed months ahead, often as soon as a venue is confirmed in late summer. Weekday parties are increasingly common, and a Tuesday or Wednesday in mid-December is usually easier to secure than the last Friday before the break. Whichever night you choose, holding it with the deposit early takes one job off the list while the rest of the plans come together.

Shared party nights and keeping it HR-friendly

Many hotels and banqueting suites run shared Christmas party nights, where several companies book tables in the same room. If you are one group among many, speak to us and to the venue before booking so everyone is clear on where the booth stands and who it is for. A neutral festive template often works best when the whole room is welcome, while a booth booked for your own tables can carry your logo.

Office parties come with their own sensitivities, so our props are festive and good-natured, and we leave out anything that could cause offence. A professional attendant stays with the booth throughout, keeps the queue moving and keeps an eye on things as the evening gets livelier. If you would rather images were not emailed or shared, sharing can be switched off and guests take prints only.

Family, friends and New Year's Eve celebrations

Not every Christmas party is a work night. We supply family gatherings, get-togethers with friends and New Year's Eve parties, whether at home or in a hired village hall, community centre or golf club function room. A booth at home needs a clear patch of floor near a socket, and the slimmer pods fit into a living room or conservatory without much rearranging.

A marquee or garden party is possible, but only with power to hand and proper cover over the booth. December weather means an open garden is never an option, so the booth must sit inside a marquee or other weatherproof structure on a level, dry floor.

Festive templates, overlays and props

Every booking includes a custom print template, and the season gives us plenty to work with: falling snow, holly, baubles, deep reds and greens, or a frosty silver and white for a more elegant evening. For New Year's Eve we can design a countdown-style overlay with clock faces, fireworks and the new year in large type. On the LCD SlimPod, smart background removal can place guests in a snowy scene with no green screen needed.

Our prop box is chosen for the season and for a mixed crowd, whether that means colleagues from every department or three generations of one family. Everything is light-hearted, easy to wear over a party outfit and quick to swap between groups.

  • Santa hats, elf ears and reindeer antlers
  • Tinsel boas and oversized novelty glasses
  • Christmas jumper and cracker joke signs
  • New Year's Eve hats, tiaras and party horns
  • No props that could embarrass or offend

Timing the booth around dinner, speeches and the disco

A typical Christmas party hire runs for three hours, and the best window is usually from the end of dinner into the disco. Opening during a sit-down meal or the speeches tends to waste hire time, because nobody leaves the table. Once plates are cleared and the speeches are done, guests are on their feet, the room loosens up and the booth stays busy until the dance floor takes over.

If your evening starts with a long drinks reception, opening early can work too, as long as you plan the hours around it. We set up in the 45 to 60 minutes before your hire window, and that time is never charged as hire time. Tell us your running order and we will suggest a start and finish to suit it.

Matching the booth to your Christmas party

The right booth depends on the room, the headcount and whether the party is for colleagues or family. All seven of our booths are available over the festive season, and these are the ones we suggest most often in December.

  • LCD SlimPod: compact, affordable, snowy virtual backdrops for smaller rooms
  • Party Booth: enclosed and curtained, up to eight festive revellers inside
  • Magic Mirror: full-length showpiece for a hotel ballroom party night
  • Inflatable Booth: lit in festive colours for a large hall
  • Retro Pod: classic photo strips, inbuilt printer, very few cables
  • GIF Booth: digital-only GIFs for internal comms and social feeds

Good to know

Christmas Parties Photo Booth FAQs

How early should we book a photo booth for a December party?

As soon as your venue and date are fixed. December weekends are claimed first, often by late summer, while weekday nights tend to stay available for longer. An £80 deposit holds the date, so you can confirm the booth early and finalise the template, timings and headcount closer to the party.

Can we brand the Christmas photos with our company logo?

Yes. Your logo can sit on every print and digital copy alongside the festive design, with a line such as the team name or a thank-you for the year. We send a proof to sign off before the night, and you can ask for changes until it looks right.

Does a booth work at a shared Christmas party night in a hotel?

It can. Talk to us and the venue first so it is clear where the booth stands and whether it is for your group or the whole room. For a whole-room booking a neutral festive template usually works best; for your own tables we can add your logo. Our attendant stays with the booth throughout.

Can you add a New Year countdown overlay?

Yes. For New Year's Eve we design a countdown-style template with clock faces, fireworks and the new year in large type, along with any names or wording you want. It appears on every print and on the digital copies guests receive by email, so the photos are clearly from that night.

What happens if the Christmas party runs late?

Plan the hire window around your latest realistic finish rather than the time on the invitation, and tell us if the timings are uncertain. If you think you may want to keep the booth going longer on the night, mention it when you book and we will tell you what is possible.

Are the props suitable for an office party?

Yes. Our props are festive and light-hearted, such as Santa hats, antlers, tinsel and seasonal signs, and we leave out anything that could offend or embarrass. The attendant stays with the booth for the whole hire, so it stays good-humoured as the night goes on.
