Cambridge, the villages and the wider county

Cambridge is the county town of Cambridgeshire and the centre of our Cambridgeshire coverage. Within the city we work in the centre, Cherry Hinton, Chesterton, Trumpington and Newnham, and we regularly head to Ely and St Ives, or across the county lines to Newmarket in Suffolk, Saffron Walden in Essex and Royston in Hertfordshire. The M11, A14, A10 and A1303 make the city easy to reach. Delivery and setup are included; if a far-flung venue ever needed a travel contribution, it would be on the quote before you commit.

Timed access, lodges and the one-socket rule

The city centre has restricted vehicle access and park and ride is no help when there is a booth to unload, so every central booking starts with a conversation about how the vehicle gets to the door. Colleges typically allow a timed delivery slot through a gated entrance, confirmed via the porters' lodge, and they want the registration in advance. We arrive inside the slot, unload at the agreed point and move the vehicle straight away.

Building the booth takes 45 to 60 minutes and is never charged as hire time. One standard socket within reach of the booth and a level floor are all we need. Lifts are rare in older buildings, so upstairs rooms mean a carried booth, and listed-building rules mean nothing is fixed to walls or taped to historic floors.

  • Delivery slot and registration agreed with the porters' lodge
  • Slim booth for narrow lanes and archways
  • No fixings, tape or cable runs on listed surfaces
  • One socket and a level floor
  • Vehicle moved immediately after unloading

College rooms, hotels and campus venues

College venues are the most distinctive: dining halls, function rooms and marquees on college lawns, reached through narrow gated entrances and lanes that were never designed for vehicles. Hotels in the city and on the outskirts host weddings and corporate dinners, riverside venues near the punting stretches are popular for summer parties, and community centres and sports clubs in Chesterton and Cherry Hinton host birthdays. The science park to the north and the biomedical campus to the south have conference facilities used for launches and company celebrations.

May Balls, graduations and science park events

May Balls and college events are planned by student committees working to tight budgets and late finishes. Unlimited sessions within the hire window mean the booth earns its place, and a slim pod that fits through an archway is usually the practical answer. Graduation parties cluster around the ceremonies and are often lunches or early-evening receptions in hotels and hired rooms, where a print booth and guest book give families something to take home.

Corporate events at the science park and the biomedical campus are a different brief altogether: branded overlays, instant digital delivery, social sharing and sometimes a green screen so that guests appear in front of a product or campaign backdrop. The venues are modern, with loading bays, lifts and ample parking, so the only question is which booth suits the brand.

Booths that fit Cambridge

The LCD SlimPod is the most requested booth in Cambridge for a simple reason: at 36 centimetres wide it fits through the narrowest lodge, needs a 1.5 by 2 metre floor space and still delivers prints, GIFs and smart background removal. The GIF Booth uses the same hardware without a printer and is the go-to for campus corporate work. The Magic Mirror is the choice for weddings and balls where the room has space for a 2-metre square and a touch of theatre.

All three come with an attendant, stick props and a template designed around your college crest, names or company logo. Each of them shares digital copies by email during the event, so a ball committee or marketing team has the full gallery the next morning without chasing anyone for it.

Good to know

Photo Booth Hire in Cambridge: Local FAQs

How do you get the booth into a Cambridge college?

We agree a timed delivery slot with the porters' lodge, supply the vehicle registration in advance and unload at the gate or courtyard the college specifies. The vehicle moves on straight away. A slim pod fits through most archways, which is why the LCD SlimPod is the usual choice.

Can you manage stairs in an old building with no lift?

Yes, as long as we know beforehand. The LCD SlimPod, GIF Booth and Retro Pod are light enough for two people to carry up a staircase. The Magic Mirror is best on the ground floor. Tell us the room's location when you book.

Is there a travel charge for Cambridge?

No. Delivery, setup and pack-down are included in every package and Cambridge is well inside our regular area. If a travel contribution ever applied to a far-flung venue, it would be shown on the quote up front, never added on the day.

Will you fix anything to the walls or floors?

No. Our booths are free-standing and need no fixings, and we do not tape cables to historic floors. Where a cable run is unavoidable we use a rubber cable protector that lifts away cleanly, which is usually acceptable to college and listed-venue managers.

Can the booth run through a May Ball until the early hours?

Yes, within the hire window agreed on the quote. Ball committees usually book the booth for the busiest hours after the first entertainment rather than the whole night, and unlimited sessions mean guests can return as often as they like within that time.
