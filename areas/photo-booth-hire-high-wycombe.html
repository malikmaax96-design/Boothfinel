M40 junctions, hills and the final few metres

Junctions 3 and 4 of the M40, the A40 and the A404 bring us into Wycombe from every direction, and we allow extra time for the town's steep roads and for weekend traffic. Banqueting suites and hotels usually have car parks and a delivery door, which keeps unloading quick. Barns and farm venues can involve a gravel track or a walk across a yard, so we ask about the last stretch in advance and plan the carry from the vehicle.

Each booth runs from one standard socket within reach, apart from the Inflatable Booth, which needs two. Our attendant builds and tests everything in 45 to 60 minutes before the hire window opens, and that time is free, never taken from your hours. If the room is upstairs, tell us whether there is a lift: the LCD SlimPod, GIF Booth and Retro Pod can be carried up a staircase, while the larger booths need a lift or level access.

Good to know

Photo Booth Hire in High Wycombe: Local FAQs

Where will you park and unload at a High Wycombe venue?

Most banqueting suites and hotels in and around the town have car parks and a delivery entrance, so we unload close to the door and then park where the venue asks. For barns and farm venues, let us know about gravel tracks, gates or a long walk from the car park so we can plan the carry.

Our function room is upstairs. Can you still set up?

Usually, yes. The LCD SlimPod, GIF Booth and Retro Pod are light enough for our team to carry up a staircase. The Magic Mirror, Party Booth and Inflatable Booth need a lift or a ground-floor room. Tell us the layout when you book and we will recommend the right booth.

Is there a travel fee for High Wycombe?

Delivery and setup are included; if any travel contribution ever applies to a far-flung venue it is shown on the quote up front. High Wycombe and the surrounding Chiltern towns sit comfortably within our regular Buckinghamshire area, so you will not find anything added on the day.

Can we book the booth for the mehndi and the reception?

Yes. Many Wycombe families book us for two or three days of the wedding, each with its own print design and running time. Send us the dates, venues and guest numbers for every event and we will put them on one quote so the whole celebration is covered.

Will the booth work in a barn with limited power points?

All our booths except the Inflatable run from one standard mains socket, and the Inflatable needs two. Barn venues normally have sockets near the main space, but it is worth checking with the venue where they are, so we can position the booth sensibly and avoid long cable runs.
