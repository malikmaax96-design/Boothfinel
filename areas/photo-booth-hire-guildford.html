Arrival, access and setup on the day

Surrey Hills venues are often reached along narrow rural lanes and finished with a long gravel drive, which slows a loaded trolley, so we build in extra time and agree the unloading point with the venue beforehand. Town-centre and university venues can have limited parking or a timed loading slot, and we work to whichever arrangement applies. If the function room is upstairs, a lift helps, though the slimmer booths are easily carried up stairs.

Our setup takes 45 to 60 minutes and is completed before the hire window starts, at no charge. One standard socket powers each booth, with two needed for the Inflatable Booth. Marquee receptions in hotel gardens or on barn lawns need a reliable 13A socket within a few metres, and if a generator supplies the tent, its provider should confirm spare capacity for the booth.

Good to know

Photo Booth Hire in Guildford: Local FAQs

Where do you unload at a country house hotel or Surrey Hills barn?

We agree the unloading point with the venue in advance, which is often a service entrance or the yard nearest the reception room. Gravel drives take a little longer with trolleys, so we allow for that, then move the van to the car park or wherever the venue asks suppliers to park.

Can a booth be taken up stairs at a university or upper-floor venue?

Yes, as long as we know beforehand. The LCD SlimPod, GIF Booth and Retro Pod carry up stairs comfortably. The Magic Mirror, Party Booth and Inflatable Booth are best placed on the ground floor or reached by lift, so please share the route from the entrance to the room when you book.

Will we pay extra for travel to a venue near Guildford?

Delivery and setup are included in every package, and Guildford, Godalming, Woking and the nearby villages fall within our usual area. If any travel contribution ever applies to a far-flung venue it is shown on the quote up front, so the figure you approve is the figure you pay.

The A3 can be slow on a Friday evening. Will the booth be ready on time?

Yes. We plan the journey around the time of day and the usual delays on the A3 and around junction 10 of the M25, then add the 45 to 60 minute setup on top. If the venue sets a supplier arrival slot, tell us when booking and we will aim for it.

Is the Inflatable Booth practical for a Guildford venue?

It suits barns, ballrooms and large function halls well. It needs a floor area of about four metres square, a ceiling of at least three metres and two standard sockets. Low-ceilinged rooms are better served by the Magic Mirror or LCD SlimPod, and we can help you compare.

How do we reserve a date for a ball or a wedding?

An £80 deposit secures the date, and the balance is due one month before the event. Summer weekends, graduation season and December are the busiest periods around Guildford, so organisers of balls, weddings and Christmas parties usually confirm several months ahead.
