Planning a booth for a large South Asian wedding

Many of our Slough bookings are South Asian weddings with 300 to 600 guests, often spread across a mehndi, a civil or religious ceremony and a reception on different days or in different halls. A few things make the booth work smoothly at that scale. First, throughput: the attendant keeps sessions moving and a mirror booth that takes eight guests per shot clears the queue faster than a four-person pod. Second, timing: open the booth after the meal, when guests are moving between tables.

Modesty and privacy are often part of the brief. The Party Booth is fully enclosed with a curtain, so ladies can take photos without being seen by the room, and we can also run a separate ladies' session with a female-only queue managed by the attendant at an agreed time. Where the celebration is split across separate halls for men and women, we can position the booth in one hall and switch at a set point in the evening. Tell us the running order and we will fit around it.

Access, unloading and setup

The large banqueting venues in and around Slough are built for deliveries, with wide doors, level access and dedicated loading areas, and most have ample parking. Hotels near Heathrow and along the A4 have service entrances and car parks; we follow the venue's instructions and move the vehicle once the kit is inside. Gurdwara and temple halls often have specific rules about shoes, timing and where equipment can go, so we ask the organiser for those in advance and respect them on the day.

Our attendant arrives about an hour before the hire window and the build takes 45 to 60 minutes, which is never billed as hire time. Most booths run from one standard socket; the Inflatable needs two. For venues where the function room is upstairs with no goods lift, let us know and we will bring a booth that can be carried.

Good to know

Photo Booth Hire in Slough: Local FAQs

Where do you park and unload at a Slough banqueting suite?

Banqueting venues around Slough have loading areas and large car parks, so we unload by the service door and park. Hotels near Heathrow and on the A4 have delivery entrances. For gurdwara and temple halls we follow the organiser's instructions on where the vehicle and equipment can go.

Can you run a ladies-only session?

Yes. We can schedule a women-only period within the hire window, with the attendant managing the queue, or use the enclosed Party Booth so that nobody outside the curtain sees the photo being taken. Tell us the running order and we will build it in.

Is there a travel fee for Slough?

No. Delivery, setup and pack-down are included and Slough is well within our regular area. If a travel contribution ever applied to a far-flung venue it would appear on the quote up front, never as an extra on the day.

Is the function room upstairs a problem?

Not if we know in advance. Most banqueting suites are on the ground floor, but some hotel rooms are upstairs. The slim pods can be carried up stairs by two people; the Magic Mirror and Inflatable Booth need a lift or a ground-floor room. Tell us the layout at booking.

Can we book the booth for more than one day of the wedding?

Yes. Many families book the booth for the mehndi and again for the reception, with a different print template for each day. Ask for a quote covering both dates and we will price them together, with an £80 deposit securing the dates.

Can the print template include text in Punjabi, Urdu or Gujarati?

Yes, as long as you supply the exact wording. We design the template with your names, date and any message in the language or languages you want, and send a proof to approve before the event.
