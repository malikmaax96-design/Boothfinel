Travel planning for our northernmost county

Being straight with planners about distance matters. Nottinghamshire is the furthest north we regularly travel, so longer journeys are scheduled into the day: the attendant leaves early, allows for M1 and A1 traffic, and still arrives 45 to 60 minutes before your hire window to build and test the booth. That setup time is never charged as hire. Delivery and setup are included as standard, and if any travel contribution applies to your venue it is confirmed and shown on the quote up front, so you know the full cost before paying a deposit.

For the same reason, we ask Nottinghamshire clients to share access details early: parking, the distance from the vehicle to the room, whether there is a lift and how many sockets sit near the proposed spot. A few minutes on the phone or WhatsApp avoids delays on the day and lets the attendant pack the right leads and bases.

Where we go in Nottinghamshire

Nottingham itself is the busiest destination, followed by West Bridgford, Beeston, Arnold, Carlton and Hucknall on the city's edges. Further out, we take bookings in Mansfield, Newark-on-Trent, Worksop, Retford and Southwell. The M1 between junctions 24 and 28 serves the west of the county, the A52 brings us into the city and across to West Bridgford, the A46 links Newark with the motorway network, and the A1 runs up the eastern side toward Retford and Worksop.

Booths that travel well and work hard

Given the distance, Nottinghamshire clients tend to favour booths that set up quickly and fit a wide range of rooms. The LCD SlimPod is the obvious starting point: slim, one socket, two prints per session plus digital copies, and the most affordable package we offer. The Selfie Mirror Pod steps up to a DSLR camera and a print for every guest, which suits weddings of around 100 to 150 people. For a full-scale wedding in a country house or city hotel, the Magic Mirror remains the showpiece, with games, animations and a guest book for messages.

Larger items such as the Inflatable Booth can still be booked for Nottinghamshire, but they need a 4m by 4m floor area, 3m of ceiling height and two sockets, so we check the venue carefully before confirming. Where a banqueting hall or hotel ballroom has the headroom, it makes a striking centrepiece for a big Nottingham celebration, and the LED glow can be matched to the colour scheme.

Typical Nottinghamshire venues

City-centre hotels and function venues in Nottingham handle most of the corporate work and a good share of weddings, and they usually have loading bays, lifts and plenty of power. Out toward Sherwood Forest and around Southwell and Newark, country houses, converted barns and village halls take over, and those bring gravel drives, uneven floors and sometimes a single socket. University events in the city, from balls to graduation celebrations, favour booths that can handle long queues with an attendant keeping things moving. Whatever the venue, we need a flat area of at least 1.5m by 2m and a socket within reach of the booth.

Event advice for weddings, parties and corporate nights

Wedding planners in Nottinghamshire usually run the booth from the end of the meal into the evening reception, and we suggest placing it where evening guests will pass on arrival. The personalised print template is proofed in advance so names and dates are right. Corporate bookings centre on Nottingham: Christmas parties, awards dinners and conference evenings, all with a branded overlay and digital copies for the comms team. Proms from schools around Nottingham, Mansfield and West Bridgford run from late June, and birthday parties in village halls and sports clubs tend to choose the SlimPod for its small footprint.

Since the journey is longer, we recommend confirming Nottinghamshire dates early, particularly summer Saturdays and December, so the schedule can be built around your event and the £80 deposit locks the date in. Midweek corporate dates and off-peak months usually have more availability, and we are happy to talk through options by phone or WhatsApp.

Good to know

Photo Booth Hire in Nottinghamshire: Local FAQs

Where will your attendant park at a Nottingham city-centre venue?

City-centre hotels usually have a loading bay or service entrance, and our attendant will unload there before moving the vehicle to a car park. Please ask the venue about unloading arrangements and any timed restrictions, and pass them on when you book, so arrival can be planned around them.

Can you set up in a marquee or a barn near Southwell or Newark?

Yes. Rural venues around Southwell, Newark and the Sherwood Forest area are a regular part of our Nottinghamshire diary. For a marquee we need a reliable 13A supply and a firm, dry floor; for a barn, a flat spot and a socket within reach of a long extension lead.

Is there a travel fee for Nottinghamshire bookings?

Delivery and setup are included in every package. Because Nottinghamshire is the furthest north we regularly travel, we check the venue location when quoting; if any travel contribution applies to a far-flung venue, it is shown on the quote up front and never added afterwards.

Do you cover university balls and student events in Nottingham?

We do. University balls, society dinners and graduation parties are popular bookings, and the attendant keeps long queues moving. The LCD SlimPod and Magic Mirror are the usual choices, with a custom print template carrying the society or event name and digital copies sent by email.

How early should a Nottinghamshire wedding be booked?

As early as you can, especially for summer Saturdays and December dates. The longer journey means we schedule Nottinghamshire bookings carefully, and an £80 deposit secures your date, with the balance due one month before the event.
