Greenwich, Lewisham, Bexley and Bromley: our South East patch

South East London for us means the four boroughs of Greenwich, Lewisham, Bexley and Bromley, running from the river at Woolwich and Greenwich down through Blackheath, Lewisham, Catford and Eltham to Sidcup, Bexleyheath and Welling, and on to Bromley, Beckenham, Chislehurst and Orpington on the Kent edge. Bromley has its own page with more local detail, and anything over the county boundary in Dartford, Sevenoaks or Swanley is covered under Kent.

Because the area sits between our London and Kent work, we are on the A2 and A20 most weeks, and a booking anywhere from Blackheath to Biggin Hill is routine for our team. Enquiries from either side of the boundary are handled the same way.

Booths that suit riverside hotels and golf clubs

Wedding receptions here are often in hotels with river views at Greenwich and Woolwich, in golf clubs across Bromley, Bexley and Chislehurst, or in Georgian halls around Blackheath, and the Magic Mirror suits all three. It is a full-length interactive mirror with animations and games, takes eight guests per shot, prints for each of them and comes with a guest book that the attendant fills as the evening goes on.

The Retro Pod is popular with couples who like the classic photo-strip look; its inbuilt printer means a single tidy unit with minimal cabling, which matters in older rooms with few sockets. For halls and proms the LCD SlimPod is the affordable all-rounder, and the Party Booth, an enclosed curtained booth for up to eight, is the one teenagers ask for by name.

The A2, the South Circular and venue access

The A2 and A20 are our main routes through the area, with the A205 South Circular linking Lewisham, Catford and Eltham, and the Blackwall Tunnel bringing us across from the east when the venue is in Greenwich or Woolwich. Evening traffic on all of them is predictable rather than dramatic, and we plan around it. Hotels and golf clubs have car parks and usually a service entrance; village halls and churches in Bexley and Bromley have on-site or street parking, and we carry the kit from van to room on trolleys.

ULEZ covers every borough in the area and we take care of it. Riverside and listed venues in Greenwich sometimes have steps at the entrance and no lift, which rules out the Magic Mirror, Party Booth and Inflatable Booth unless the room is on the ground floor; the slim pods are carried up without fuss. Our setup takes 45 to 60 minutes before the hire window, is never charged as hire time, and needs a single standard socket, or two for the Inflatable Booth.

Where South East London celebrates

Venues here split neatly by district. Greenwich and Woolwich have riverside hotels, converted naval and industrial buildings and university-adjacent halls; Blackheath has Georgian houses and church halls; Lewisham and Catford have community centres, pubs and school halls; Eltham, Sidcup, Bexleyheath and Welling have social clubs, golf clubs and hotels on the A2 corridor; and Bromley, Beckenham, Chislehurst and Orpington have golf clubs, hotels and village halls that feel more Kent than London.

Marquees in gardens around Chislehurst and Orpington are common in summer, and they work well for any booth provided the floor is solid and level and there is power within reach of a standard extension lead. If the marquee has a dance floor laid, that is usually the best spot for the booth.

Weddings, proms and Christmas dos on the Kent side

Weddings are the biggest part of our South East London diary, and a planner's first job is to check the room height: the Magic Mirror needs 2m of clearance and the Inflatable 3m, which is rarely an issue in a hotel but worth a glance in an older hall. Proms for schools across Bexley and Bromley mostly happen in late June in hotel suites or golf clubs. Birthdays fill village halls, pub rooms and sports clubs, and a growing number of 1st and 2nd birthdays book a booth as much for the parents as the children.

Corporate Christmas parties concentrate around Greenwich, Bromley town centre and the business parks along the A2. Asian weddings in the area are fewer than in West or East London but no less welcome; for a reception finishing late, tell us the end time and we will quote the hire window to match.

Good to know

Photo Booth Hire in South East London: Local FAQs

Where do you park at a Greenwich riverside venue?

Most riverside hotels and halls in Greenwich and Woolwich have a service entrance or a short-stay unloading spot, and we use that before moving the van to a car park. If your venue has specific delivery instructions or a timed window, send them over and we will plan around them.

The function room is up a staircase with no lift. Can you still set up?

Yes, with one of the slim pods: the LCD SlimPod, GIF Booth, Retro Pod and Selfie Mirror Pod are all carried up stairs. The Magic Mirror, Party Booth and Inflatable Booth need a lift or a ground-floor room, so mention the staircase when you enquire and we will advise.

Do you charge for travel to Orpington, Biggin Hill or the Kent border?

No. Delivery, setup and pack-down are included throughout Greenwich, Lewisham, Bexley and Bromley and just over the Kent boundary. If a travel contribution ever applies to a far-flung venue, it is shown on the quote up front, never added on the day.

Does ULEZ affect our booking?

Not in any way you will notice. ULEZ covers all four boroughs in the area and we take care of it as part of free delivery. There is nothing for you to pay, register or declare, whatever vehicle we bring on the day.

Can a school book a booth for a prom at a golf club or hotel?

Yes. Schools and prom committees across Bexley and Bromley book with us every summer. We send our public liability certificate for the school's records, work with the venue on access and timings, and the attendant keeps the queue orderly all evening.
