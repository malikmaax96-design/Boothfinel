Country houses, barns and conference hotels: the Northamptonshire venue mix

Northamptonshire is known for its country houses and estate barns, and a large share of the weddings we attend here take place in them. Expect sweeping drives, ballrooms with high ceilings and plenty of character, along with the occasional long walk from the car park. Around Northampton and Kettering, hotels and conference venues take care of corporate dinners, Christmas parties and larger Asian weddings, while banqueting halls in Northampton cater for celebrations of several hundred guests.

The Silverstone area draws motorsport-themed corporate hospitality and launches, where branded digital content is in demand. Across the rest of the county, village halls in and around Oundle, Towcester, Brackley and Desborough host birthdays, anniversaries and christenings, and the Rockingham Forest area adds woodland venues and farm barns to the list.

Weddings, Asian weddings and corporate events: planner notes

Country house weddings here often have a drinks reception on the lawn and the booth indoors for the evening, so we advise setting the hire window to open as guests move inside after the meal. Send us the names, date and any colour scheme and we will build the print template and share a proof.

Northampton's Asian wedding venues host receptions where several hundred guests want photos over a few hours. A high-capacity booth, an attendant managing the queue and a template carrying both families' names keep things flowing. For multi-day events, we quote each day separately with its own design, and the £80 deposit secures the dates.

Corporate clients book for awards nights, conference dinners, Silverstone-area hospitality and December parties, with a logo overlay and digital copies included. Proms from schools in Northampton, Kettering and Wellingborough arrive from late June, and the SlimPod or Party Booth suits most hotel function rooms used for them.

Access, setup and power across the county

Estate venues mean gravel drives, sometimes a long carry from the parking area and floors that are rarely level in older barns. Our attendant brings a trolley, a levelling base and extension leads to cope with all three. Upstairs function rooms in hotels and clubs are fine with a lift or a reasonable staircase for most models, though the Inflatable Booth should stay at ground level. A single standard socket powers most booths; the inflatable needs two.

We allow 45 to 60 minutes before the hire window for setup and testing, and that time is not charged as hire. Marquee receptions on country estates need a reliable 13A supply and a firm, dry floor under cover. Delivery and setup are included throughout Northamptonshire, and anything unusual about a venue is reflected on the quote before you commit.

Good to know

Photo Booth Hire in Northamptonshire: Local FAQs

Where can the attendant park and unload at a Northampton banqueting venue?

Banqueting venues in Northampton usually have a service entrance and dedicated parking, and we will unload there before moving the vehicle. For hotels and country houses, please ask the venue about the closest unloading point to the function room and let us know when booking so the attendant plans the carry.

Can you set up in a marquee on a Northamptonshire country estate?

Yes. Marquee weddings on estates around the county are a regular booking. We need a reliable 13A supply, a firm and dry floor, and a spot away from the entrance. If the power comes from a generator, let us know so we can confirm the booth is suited to it.

Do you charge travel for venues in Oundle or Brackley?

Delivery and setup are included across Northamptonshire. If any travel contribution ever applies to a far-flung venue, it is shown on the quote up front, before any deposit is paid, so the figure you agree is the figure you pay.

Which booth is best for a large Asian wedding in Northampton?

The Inflatable Booth is the usual choice, with room for ten guests per shot and a glow matched to the decor, provided the venue has a 4m by 4m area and 3m of ceiling height. The Magic Mirror is a strong alternative where space is tighter, taking up to eight guests.

Do you work at corporate hospitality events near Silverstone?

We do. Motorsport-themed hospitality, product launches and client days in the Silverstone area often choose the GIF Booth or LCD SlimPod with a branded overlay, so guests leave with shareable content. Access to temporary structures is confirmed with the organiser at quote stage.
