Asian weddings, Heathrow conferences and Fulham parties

Asian weddings dominate the West London calendar. Sikh, Hindu and Muslim celebrations in Southall, Hounslow, Harrow, Wembley and Hayes often spread across several days, and the booth is usually booked for the reception, the sangeet or mehndi night, or both. Guest counts of 400 to 600 are normal, so the practical advice is to choose a booth that takes eight to ten people per shot, pick a package with prints for every guest, and give the attendant a clear finish time, because receptions here run late.

Corporate work clusters around the Heathrow hotels in Hillingdon and Hounslow, where conferences, awards nights and Christmas parties book from September, and around the offices of Hammersmith and Chiswick. Proms for schools across Ealing, Harrow and Hillingdon land in late June. Birthday parties are everywhere from Southall community halls to Fulham bars, and summer garden parties in Ealing and Chiswick often go for a marquee.

Showpiece booths for big West London guest lists

The Inflatable Booth is the West London showpiece: a 2.5m LED-lit enclosure that fits ten guests, prints for every one of them and glows in a colour matched to the mandap or the decor. It needs 4m by 4m of floor and 3m of headroom, which the big banqueting halls of Southall and Wembley provide easily. The 360 Booth is the other big request for Asian receptions and Heathrow corporate events, with slow-motion video and instant sharing; we confirm its space and power needs for your venue at quote stage.

The Magic Mirror continues to be the all-round wedding favourite, and the Selfie Mirror Pod gives a mirror experience with a DSLR camera and prints for each guest in a compact 2m by 2m footprint, which suits hotel suites and the smaller function rooms of Chiswick and Fulham.

Banqueting halls, airport hotels and riverside pubs

Purpose-built banqueting halls with stages and dance floors line the main roads through Southall, Hayes, Harrow and Wembley, and gurdwara, temple and mosque community halls host many events too. The Heathrow belt in Hillingdon and Hounslow is wall-to-wall hotels and conference centres, Uxbridge and Ruislip have golf clubs and hotels on the Buckinghamshire edge, and Chiswick, Hammersmith and Fulham have riverside pubs, Victorian halls, rowing clubs and private members' spaces.

Banqueting halls almost always have wide doors, ground-floor access and ample power, so every booth in our range works in them. Community halls can be busier with other activities on the day, so it helps to agree a setup time with the hall manager in advance.

Ealing to Uxbridge: the boroughs we cover

The West London area on this site covers the boroughs of Ealing, Hounslow, Hillingdon, Brent, Harrow and Hammersmith and Fulham, along with the western side of Kensington and Chelsea. Districts we visit most include Ealing, Acton, Chiswick, Hammersmith, Fulham, Hounslow, Southall, Hayes, Uxbridge, Ruislip, Harrow, Wembley and Greenford. Slough and Windsor across the Berkshire border have their own pages, and venues in Watford and Rickmansworth sit under Hertfordshire.

Brent and Harrow venues on the North Circular are equally close to our North London coverage; whichever page you found first, the quote and the service are the same. Just tell us the venue postcode and we will take it from there.

The M4, the A40 and the loading bay

The M4 and A4 from Hammersmith to Heathrow, the A40 through Acton and Greenford to Uxbridge, and the A406 past Wembley are our routes, and the first two are heavy in the evening peak, so we schedule arrival accordingly. Heathrow hotels usually have a service yard and a loading bay with a booking system, and some ask for a vehicle registration and a copy of our insurance in advance; we supply both. Banqueting halls have car parks, and Chiswick and Fulham pubs mostly mean street unloading and moving the van.

ULEZ covers all of West London and is included in our free delivery. Where a room is upstairs without a lift, the LCD SlimPod, GIF Booth, Retro Pod and Selfie Mirror Pod are carried up; the Magic Mirror, Party Booth and Inflatable Booth need a lift or a ground-floor room. Each booth needs one standard socket (the Inflatable two), and our 45 to 60 minutes of setup before the hire window is never charged as hire time.

Good to know

Photo Booth Hire in West London: Local FAQs

Where do you unload at a Southall or Wembley banqueting hall?

Banqueting halls almost always have a car park or a service entrance, and we unload directly from there into the hall. If the venue has a specific supplier entrance or a time slot for deliveries, tell us and we will arrive within it with the kit on trolleys.

Our hotel function room is on the first floor. Do you need a lift?

For the Magic Mirror, Party Booth and Inflatable Booth, yes: they need a lift or a ground-floor room. The LCD SlimPod, GIF Booth, Retro Pod and Selfie Mirror Pod can be carried up stairs. Most hotels have a goods lift, which makes every booth an option.

Is there a travel charge for Heathrow hotels or Uxbridge?

No. Delivery, setup and pack-down are included across all of West London, Heathrow included. If a travel contribution ever applies to a far-flung venue, it is shown on the quote up front, and nothing is added afterwards.

Can the booth stay until 1am for a wedding reception?

Yes, as long as the venue permits suppliers on site at that hour. Asian receptions in the area often finish late and we quote the hire window to match your real finish time, with pack-down planned around the hall's closing rules.

Can prints be branded for a corporate event at a Heathrow hotel?

Yes. A custom print and overlay template with your text and logo is included with every booking, and digital copies can be delivered by email or QR for guests to share. Send your logo files and brand colours and we will produce a proof to approve.

Does the venue need your insurance certificate before you arrive?

Many Heathrow hotels and conference centres ask for it, along with a vehicle registration for the loading bay. We hold public liability insurance and send the certificate as soon as you request it, so pass on the venue contact and we will handle it.
